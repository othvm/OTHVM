# Daily Plan

Use when the operator asks for a daily plan, morning briefing, prioritization, or a review of today.

## Gather (parallel where possible)
1. `calendar` list today and tomorrow, Africa/Cairo.
2. `tasks` `google` list open tasks with due dates; check `tasks` `local` and `tasks` `linear` only if Google is unavailable or the operator uses them.
3. `info` weather today + prayer times.
4. `memory` `context` latest and `memory` `db` for active commitments.

## Build
- Fixed events first, including travel and prayer breaks.
- Pick three outcomes that actually fit the free time; do not fill every hour.
- Put deep work in the highest-energy block; batch small errands.
- Leave 30–60 minutes of buffer and a realistic stop time.
- Flag one risk: overbooking, travel time, missing info, or a deadline.

## Reply
Egyptian Arabic, six to ten lines:
1. The three outcomes.
2. The next fixed event and when to leave.
3. One risk and one suggestion.
4. Offer to schedule the outcomes as tasks or events.

## Act
- Creating the operator's own task or calendar event is T1; just do it after saying so.
- Inviting other people, moving their events, or sending the plan externally is T2.
- Save the plan to `memory` only if it creates a commitment or the operator asks.
- Never invent tasks, events, weather, or prayer times.
